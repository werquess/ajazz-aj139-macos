# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Assign actions, keyboard shortcuts or macros to the middle and side buttons
- Macro editor with shortcuts, typed text and pauses
- Smooth scrolling, reversed mouse scroll direction, scroll speed and precise scrolling with Option
- Menu bar item with battery level, DPI stage switching and scroll toggles
- Keeps running in the background, optional Dock icon and open at login
- Buttons, macros and scrolling also work over Bluetooth

## 1.0.1

- Recognize the mouse when it is connected over Bluetooth and explain that battery level and DPI need the USB cable or the 2.4 GHz receiver

## 1.0.0

- Battery level and USB power state
- Six DPI stages from 50 to 26000 DPI
- Active DPI stage selection
- Settings with language, battery refresh interval and support link
- English and Turkish interface
